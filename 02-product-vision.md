# 02 — Product vision

## A broad opportunity

Imagine a learner who wants to understand a topic but is not yet confident about where to begin. An AI-powered learning experience could help by making ideas easier to explore, showing relationships, responding to questions, and adapting the explanation to the learner's needs.

Your task is to decide what valuable experience to create within this broad opportunity.

## Questions worth exploring

These are optional prompts to help you form a direction:

- What makes a difficult concept easier to understand?
- When is a diagram, animation, worked example, analogy, simulation, or spoken explanation more useful than a paragraph?
- How could a learner ask questions or influence the direction of a lesson?
- How might the experience recognise confusion or misconceptions?
- What should happen when the AI is uncertain or gives an unhelpful explanation?
- How could a learner revisit, practise, or apply what they have learned?
- What would make the experience accessible and comfortable to use?

You do not need to answer every question or implement every possibility.

## Possible directions (illustrative only)

An applicant might focus on one carefully designed topic lesson, a visual explanation tool, an interactive problem-solving tutor, a voice-led teaching experience, a collaborative canvas, or a specialised tutor for a particular learner group. These are examples, not approved categories or required features. An original direction is welcome.

## Product judgement

Make your target user and core value clear. Explain why the chosen interaction is appropriate for that learner and topic. Show how the experience helps someone make progress, rather than relying only on an attractive interface or an AI chat response.
