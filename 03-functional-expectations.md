# 03 — Functional expectations

## No mandatory feature checklist

This assignment deliberately does not prescribe a fixed list of screens, controls, or features. Choose the functionality that best supports the product direction you have selected.

The submitted experience should provide enough working behaviour for a reviewer to understand and evaluate your idea. A static mock-up or a collection of screenshots alone is unlikely to demonstrate the complete experience.

## What counts as a meaningful experience?

Depending on your approach, meaningful behaviour could include a learner entering a topic, exploring a visual explanation, manipulating an example, asking a follow-up question, receiving feedback, or progressing through a learning activity. These are examples only; you are free to design a different interaction.

## AI behaviour and transparency

If your product presents a capability as AI-powered, make its actual behaviour clear. Distinguish live model responses from hard-coded examples, mocked API responses, prerecorded audio, or simulated interactions. If you use a hybrid approach, explain which parts are live and which are simulated.

You are not required to use a particular model, provider, or AI technique. The quality and appropriateness of your AI integration matter more than the provider's name.

## Scope and completeness

Choose a scope you can execute thoughtfully. It is acceptable to leave some ideas out. Document important limitations, known issues, and any parts that are demonstrations rather than complete product functionality.
