# 01 — Assignment overview

## Purpose

This assignment gives applicants a shared, open-ended product and engineering challenge. It is designed to reveal how you understand a problem, decide what matters, explore possible solutions, and deliver a working result.

## Challenge area

Create an AI-powered interactive learning experience inspired by the idea of a whiteboard tutor. The broad opportunity is to help someone learn a topic in a way that feels understandable, engaging, and useful.

The phrase *interactive whiteboard tutor* is a starting point, not a restriction. You may interpret it as a visual teaching space, a conversational tutor, a guided problem-solving environment, a simulation, or another coherent learning product.

## Define your own direction

You are responsible for deciding:

- Who the intended learner is and what learning situation you are addressing.
- Which learning problem you want to solve.
- What the experience should feel like and how a learner uses it.
- Which capabilities are essential to your chosen direction.
- Which technical approach best supports that experience.
- What you can deliver well within the time and resources available to you.

You may narrow the scope substantially. A small, complete experience with a clear purpose can be stronger than a broad collection of unfinished features.

## What matters

We want to understand the thinking behind the work: why you chose the problem, how you arrived at your approach, what trade-offs you made, what works, and what you would improve next.

There is no reference implementation and no preferred stack. Different applicants may submit very different products and still demonstrate strong work.
