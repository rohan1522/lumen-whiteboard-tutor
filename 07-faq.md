# 07 — Frequently asked questions

### Is there a fixed time limit?

No fixed development time limit is specified in this assignment. Choose a scope you can complete thoughtfully and submit when you are ready.

### Do I have to build the exact product shown in the assignment title?

No. The title describes the broad challenge area. You may interpret the idea in your own way, provided you explain the product and the problem it addresses.

### Are any features compulsory?

No fixed feature checklist is required. Select the capabilities that best support your chosen experience. The result should be meaningfully explorable so reviewers can understand what you built.

### Do I have to use a specific programming language, framework, or AI provider?

No. Choose the stack and services that make sense for your solution and explain important decisions.

### Can I use AI coding assistants?

Yes. You may use AI coding assistants and other development tools. You remain responsible for the submitted work and should be able to explain its important parts, decisions, and limitations.

### Can I use mock data or simulated AI responses?

You may use them where appropriate, especially for parts of a prototype. Clearly identify mocked, sample, prerecorded, or simulated behaviour and do not present it as a live AI capability.

### Does a static prototype count?

A static mock-up alone may not provide enough evidence to evaluate the experience. Aim to provide a working, meaningfully explorable demonstration of your chosen idea.

### Must the demo be publicly accessible?

Provide a live demo URL reviewers can access. If access is restricted, explain the requirements clearly in your submission and provide a practical way to review the project.

### Will submission guarantee an interview or job?

No. The assignment is one part of the selection process and does not guarantee an interview, offer, or employment.

### Who owns the submitted work?

This assignment does not by itself transfer ownership of your work to Braynix Studios. Any separate commercial use, licensing, or transfer of rights would need to be discussed and agreed in writing.

### Where do I submit?

Share your GitHub repository URL and live demo URL, then complete the [official submission form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdFJoTxMEuFZrCHTbEgv7bbg2uLf33SjP7n0w2qUONR4kMRcA/viewform?usp=publish-editor).
